### Field 4: The Idea (SymboLM: Adaptive Symbolic Reasoning)
Current reasoning models (such as DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI o1) suffer from two core inefficiencies:
1. **The Verbosity Tax:** They waste up to 75% of their inference tokens generating verbose English chain-of-thought for elementary steps where dense algebraic manipulation is superior.
2. **The Cold-Start Exploration Barrier:** Pure reinforcement learning cannot discover formal domain languages out of thin air because the probability of randomly sampling exact multi-token syntax from a 150,000-token vocabulary is practically zero.

To solve this, I designed **SymboLM**:
- **Symbolic Reasoning Language (SRL v1.0):** A domain-specific formal grammar executed via an AST parser and verified at runtime by SymPy.
- **Tri-Register Cognitive Architecture:** Dynamic compute allocation routing queries to:
  - `<reg:deduce>`: Dense symbolic execution for multi-step mathematics.
  - `<reg:bypass>`: Zero-shot direct answers (0 thinking tokens) for factual memory.
  - `<reg:intent>`: 10-token scratchpads for empathetic dialogue.
- **2-Phase Alignment Bridge:** Solved the cold-start exploration barrier by first performing Syntactic SFT Seeding (teaching `<reg:plan>`, `<reg:deduce>`, `<reg:verify>` across 7 Olympiad math families), followed by GRPO reinforcement learning to prune noise and maximize accuracy.

Working entirely from free-tier cloud GPUs on Kaggle, I have successfully trained and verified the Stage 2.5 Concept SFT policy adapter and released open checkpoints on Hugging Face.
